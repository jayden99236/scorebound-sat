# Scorebound SAT Practice

A free, single-page practice site in the digital SAT format.

- 209 original questions (84 Reading and Writing, 125 Math), tagged Easy, Medium, or Hard
- Question bank organized by topic and subtopic, with difficulty and correct/missed filters
- Weak-spot tracking: one-click 5-question tests on the subtopics you miss most
- Section practice, mixed drills, and a full-length test with an adaptive Module 2
- SAT-style timing: hideable timer, 5-minute warning, auto-submit, auto-resuming break, optional +50% time, and per-question pacing
- Estimated section and total scores with a breakdown by skill domain
- Hover explanations in practice, plus a full answer review
- English, Russian, or Uzbek interface (Math questions translated too), with an EN / RU / UZ switch inside every explanation

Everything runs in the browser from `index.html`; history and language choice are saved in your browser only.

Scores are rough estimates, not an official conversion. Questions are original and not taken from any real test.
